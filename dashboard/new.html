                    How 72 Ghante works

                    1. Farmer reportsSends a voice note or message on WhatsApp, or calls, in Marathi, Hindi or Marwadi.
                    2. 72-hour clock startsThe agent reads when the damage happened and starts the PMFBY 72-hour countdown.
                    3. Details + photo checkFinds the policy from the phone number or a photo of the slip, asks only what is missing, checks crop photos.
                    4. Farmer says yesReads everything back and files only after the farmer confirms.
                    5. Files and verifiesPicks the best of 6 official routes, files, and checks the docket really exists before saying "done".
                    6. Never gives upIf the portal fails it retries, switches route, and escalates to the district office before time runs out.
